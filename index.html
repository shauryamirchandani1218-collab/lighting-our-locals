About Project LOL

What is Project LOL?

Project LOL, which stands for Lighting Our Locals, is a community problem-solving project focused on inadequate street lighting in Bothell, Washington. The project connects pedestrian safety, public awareness, and community reporting to help identify areas where lighting concerns may affect residents.

The Problem

Poor lighting can make sidewalks, neighborhoods, parks, and walking paths feel unsafe, especially during early morning and evening hours. Many pedestrians rely on safe visibility when walking to school, exercising, commuting, or moving through their community.

How the App Helps

This web platform allows users to report lighting issues by entering a location, selecting an issue type, rating the safety concern, adding a description, and uploading a photo. Reports can then be reviewed through an admin dashboard and updated with a status.

Key Project Evidence

41 community survey responses collected.

83% of respondents reported walking during early morning or evening hours.

79% of pedestrian deaths occur at night.

Project actions included surveys, outreach materials, city communication, Customer Action Requests, and the development of this reporting tool.
